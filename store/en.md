## Descrição completa (1196/4000)

Can you sing it backwards?

Record a phrase, a song or an impression. The game plays it backwards. You copy that weird sound, and the game reverses your copy. Nail it and you'll hear the original phrase in your own voice. Miss it and you'll hear a brand-new language, and a lot of laughing.

GUESS IT, WITH FRIENDS
• 2 to 8 players, each with their own name
• One player secretly records anything: a phrase, a chorus, a singer or an influencer
• The next player hears it backwards, copies it and guesses what it was
• Drum roll, the big reveal, confetti and a winner at the end

SOLO
• Phrases and tongue twisters to practise with
• A score from 0 to 100 for every try
• Beat your best score

SHARE IT
• Make a vertical video with the original, the backwards version and your attempt
• Ready for TikTok, Instagram and WhatsApp

EASY TO PLAY
• Slower backwards audio (0.75×) so you can actually copy it
• Silence at the start and end of recordings is trimmed for you
• Pick a different microphone if your phone's default one acts up
• In English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, Italian and German

PRIVATE
No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Your audio stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere.
